# Bibliography

Theory behind the route solver. The papers are **not** included in this
repository (they belong to their publishers); search the exact titles on Google
Scholar, arXiv, or your university library.

## Foundations used by CoverTheMap

| Topic | Reference |
|-------|-----------|
| Minimum-weight matching (the heart of the exact solver) | Edmonds, J. *Paths, Trees, and Flowers*. Canadian Journal of Mathematics, 1965. |
| Chinese Postman via matching + Euler tours | Edmonds, J. & Johnson, E. L. *Matching, Euler Tours and the Chinese Postman*. Mathematical Programming, 1973. |
| Surveys of arc routing | Eiselt, Gendreau & Laporte. *Arc Routing Problems, Part I: The Chinese Postman Problem*. Operations Research, 1995. <br> Eiselt, Gendreau & Laporte. *Arc Routing Problems, Part II: The Rural Postman Problem*. Operations Research, 1995. |
| Blossom implementation we ported | Joris van Rantwijk, *Maximum Weighted Matching* (`mwmatching.py`), the same code NetworkX uses. |
